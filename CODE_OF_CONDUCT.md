# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Use inclusive language.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on the issue rather than the person.
- Respect different opinions and backgrounds.
- Help maintain a friendly technical community.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Spam or unrelated promotional content.
- Any conduct that may reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.
